Set up

  1. Phone on a stand, landscape, behind and a little to the side of where you shoot, about hip to shoulder height.
  2. Whole goal in view with about one goal-width of space around it. The stand must not move.
  3. Mark the 4 inner corners of the goal opening once.
  4. Shoot at least 2 seconds apart. Dark shoes help (white shoes can look like a ball).
  5. Keep the page open and the phone plugged in. The screen stays on while tracking.

Runs only on this phone. Nothing is uploaded and no video is saved. A few still images of unsure shots stay in memory until you close the page.

Tap the top-left corner Inner corner of the goal opening. Step 1 of 4. Drag a dot to adjust.
